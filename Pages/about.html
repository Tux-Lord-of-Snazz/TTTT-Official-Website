About

It Takes Two To Tunnel is a retro, platformer-like defence game with a focus on multiplayer co-op and teamwork.

Grab a friend to help you fend off the oncoming hordes of golems as you descend deeper into the mines in search of your Dwarven King, defending your fortress from harm as you go. Choose from a selection of characters and utilise their powerful combo attack abilities to stop the golems. You’ll need all the help you can get, because after all, It Takes Two To Tunnel!

Meet The Dwarves

Fighter

The fighter is a powerful Dwarf that uses a plethora of different axes to annihilate those pesky golems. From your standard Rock Splitter to Bouncing Chakrams, he really has it all. And don’t forget about his powerful Meg-AXE, either.

Valkyrie

The Valkyrie specialises in defending, using shields to bash golems out of her path and protect her allies. While she moves a little bit slower, she makes up for it with extra defence and a speedy shield dash.

Thorin

Thorin is the demi-god of 'Powerful Electricity from the Sky'. Unfortunately the 'God of Thunder' was already taken. Nonetheless, his powers come in handy when fighting off the golems, and his hammer is always up for some smashing. He can play a wicked solo on the electric guitar, too.

Meet The Golems

Basic Golem

The simplest Golem there is and the primary ‘oomph’ behind the Golem Assault. He may be basic, but don't let him fool you, he’s smarter than he looks and packs a mean punch!

Golem Brute

These hulking golems are mean, grumpy, disgruntled, and downright angry. So watch out, they carry a Powerfist capable of launching dwarves into the upper stratosphere - I’m told the fist hurts almost as much as the landing.

Pebbles

The Golem Army’s weapon of choice, these nasty little monsters come in droves and move like the wind. While they hit like a marshmallow and smash just as easily, be careful not to get overwhelmed, they’ll show no mercy.
